The order starts as proposed. Nothing is binding and no money moves until both parties accept the terms in the app and pay through the authorised partner.

Safeguards built into the flow

  • Delivery inside 3 days cannot be posted as an early order - that is a spot sale, and pretending otherwise hides risk.
  • A listing marked low-confidence for quantity warns the farmer to commit only what they are sure about.
  • Crop failure is a documented reschedule or partial-supply path, not an instant repayment demand.
  • Every status change writes an event with an actor, so disputes are read from a record.
  • Quantity that is already committed is removed from what a farmer can promise again.

What the money line actually means

Advance = quantity × agreed price × deposit %. Balance = total − advance. Platform fee = total × commission %, disclosed before acceptance. Short delivery is priced at the agreed rate for what was actually supplied, so nobody is penalised twice and nobody overpays. Settlement maths live in app/engine/earlyorders.py.
