How matching is decided

Weights: proximity 30 %, quantity 25 %, timing 20 %, price 15 %, grade 10 %. A greedy one-pass allocation lets one listing serve several buyers until its uncommitted quantity runs out, and fresh matooke is only treated as a fit if harvest lands within 21 days of the needed date. District centroids are approximate and used for ordering only, never for navigation.
